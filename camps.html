A day at camp

What a typical day looks like

Structured but never rigid. Plenty of building time, breaks for movement, and a share at the end of every day.

🌤️

Half-day camp

9:00 am – 12:30 pm

9:00

Welcome & warm-up

Arrival, name games and an unplugged logic challenge to wake up the brain.

9:20

Concept of the day

One new idea, demonstrated live and explored together as a group.

9:50

Build time

Students work on their own project with teacher support and pair help.

10:35

Break & snack

Outdoor play, snack and screen-free time.

11:00

Build time 2

Add features, fix bugs, make it better.

11:40

Team challenge

A quick group puzzle, competition or design challenge.

12:10

Show & share

Everyone demos what they made today. Pack up and pick-up.

☀️

Full-day camp

9:00 am – 3:30 pm

9:00

Morning block

Same structure as half-day camp: warm-up, concept, build time, break, build time.

12:30

Lunch & outdoor play

A proper break — food, running around and recharging.

1:15

Afternoon project

A second, deeper build — often a new skill layered on the morning's work.

2:00

Unplugged & physical activity

Robotics, board games, coding tag or team challenges away from screens.

2:45

Demo preparation

Polish, practise presenting and prepare for Friday's showcase.

3:15

Showcase & pick-up

Daily share, certificates for effort, and handover to parents.

FAQ

Camp questions

Does my child need any coding experience?

No. Every camp starts from the beginning. Junior Coders needs no reading or typing at all.

What should my child bring?

A water bottle, a hat, and a packed snack if they have dietary requirements. Everything else — laptops, tablets, robots and materials — is provided. Full-day campers get lunch, but many parents still send extra snacks.

What time is drop-off and pick-up?

Drop-off from 8:45 am. Half-day pick-up is at 12:30 pm; full-day pick-up is at 3:30 pm. Late pick-up can be arranged for a small fee.

How many children are in each camp?

Maximum 10, with two teachers on hand. Junior Coders and AI & Robotics camps are capped at 8.

Can my child attend just one day?

Camps are designed as a five-day arc, so we don't offer single days. If you need flexibility, look at our weekly term-time classes instead.

What if my child misses a day?

We'll help them catch up the next morning, and send home the project files so they can continue at home. Camp fees are not refunded for missed days.

Is lunch provided?

Full-day campers receive a hot lunch and afternoon snack. Half-day campers get a morning snack and juice. Let us know about allergies when you book.

What's the cancellation policy?

[E.g. Full refund up to 7 days before camp starts; 50% refund within 7 days; no refund after camp begins.]

Can you run a private camp for our group?

Yes — for groups of 4 or more we can arrange a private camp at your home, school or our venue. Contact us for a quote.
